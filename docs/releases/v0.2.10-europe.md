Fix: mission timers on the European disc no longer run faster above 30 fps

- European disc: a mission's timer or bar went faster the higher your frame rate was, so at 60 fps or more you had less time than intended and some missions could not be finished - the only way through was to lock the game to 30 fps.
- The missions concerned are the ones that move a gauge every frame: Boomshine Blowout (the heat), Hose the Hoes (the girls' bar), Balls (the havoc bar), The Exchange (the TIME bar), Kill Phil (Phil's health), Papi Don't Screech (the stress bar), the fire helicopter mission (the bucket's water and the helicopter's health), Burning Bridges (the pressure), the driving range and Home's on the Range (the aim), Over the Top (the searchlights) and Robbing the Cradle (the distances).
- These gauges move by a fixed amount on every frame of the game's script, and on a PS2 a frame is always a thirtieth of a second. They now follow the game's clock instead, so they run at the same speed at 60, 100 or 144 frames a second as at 30: the heat of Boomshine Blowout rises 0.45 a second at any of them (it rose 0.9 a second at 60 and 2.16 at 144 before this release).
- At 30 fps, the game's original frame rate, nothing changes.

Updating

- Extract this release into a short local folder, start `VCSLauncher.exe`, choose your own USA or European disc and build the game. No game code or game data is included.
- Existing saves are kept outside the release folder. The first build after an update can take several minutes; later builds only compile changed files.
- On Linux, download the AppImage when it appears on this release.
