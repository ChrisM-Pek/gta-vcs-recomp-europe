Mods: install, switch and remove them

- The launcher's **Mods** tab now keeps your mods as a list: one row each, with a switch that turns it on or off and **Remove** beside it. Hovering a row tells you what the mod is, how much it holds and where it came from.
- Switching a mod off puts its files aside instead of deleting them, so turning it back on is instant; the game reads whichever mods are on when it starts.
- **Install a mod...** takes a texture pack's `.zip` or `.7z` (both are offered now - before, a `.7z` pack could not be picked in the dialog). The pack is put next to the game and switched on.
- In the game, the HD textures switch has its own place: the settings (F2) have a new **Mods** page, with **Texture packs** and **HD textures (HUD)** - PS2 (original) or HD pack. Before, it sat under Graphics > Image quality.
- Nothing of the game or of a pack is included: the Mods tab links the HD HUD pack's page on GTAForums, and a pack belongs to its author.

Updating

- Extract this release into a short local folder, start `VCSLauncher.exe`, choose your own USA or European disc and build the game. No game code or game data is included.
- Existing saves are kept outside the release folder. The first build after an update can take several minutes; later builds only compile changed files.
- On Linux, download the AppImage when it appears on this release.
