New: the original project's 0.3.0, on the European disc

- This release brings everything the original repository released as 0.3.0 to the European (SLES-54622) build: the effects at high frame rates, the radio, the settings changes, the mission fixes and the crash and memory fixes below. Nothing of it is US-only any more.
- The European disc's addresses for all of it were matched against the game's European executable and checked one by one, so every fix works on the same code it does on the American disc. See the last section for what still needs playing through.

Effects at high frame rates

- Above 30 fps, smoke, exhaust, tyre smoke, spray, rain splashes and dust faded far too fast, and far too many were made. At 144 fps smoke lasted a fifth as long, and fires looked thin. They now look and last as on the PS2 at any frame rate.
- Lightning, clouds, camera shake and explosions also ran faster at high frame rates. Storms flickered in short bursts, clouds turned three times too fast, camera shake buzzed instead of shaking, and explosions were over sooner. They now run at the PS2's pace.
- Above 30 fps, rain came and went in waves: a second or so of rain, then about three seconds of none, over and over for minutes. It now falls as steadily as on the PS2. As on the PS2, rain that has just started can come and go for a few seconds before it settles, and it still turns with the camera.
- The light trails behind cars and street lights at night were gone above 30 fps. They're back, and a quarter weaker above 30 fps or above the original resolution, where they stood out more than they did on the PS2.
- If you preferred the effects as they were before, the game's settings (F2) → Enhancements → Effects: **Reduce Post Processing Effects** puts all of this back at once. It's off by default and applies straight away.

The radio

- While you drive, the mouse wheel now changes the radio station: down is the next station, up the one before.
- It's part of the game's settings (F2) → Keyboard: **Mouse wheel**, which is on by default. If you've bound the wheel to a control, it keeps doing only that.
- While the radio plays an announcement the game doesn't let you change the station, with the wheel or any other way.
- Above 30 fps, getting back into a car put the station further on than it should have been. The time you'd spent on foot counted double at 60 fps and almost five times at 144. The station now picks up where it would on the PS2.

Settings

- The game now starts in full screen (borderless, on the monitor it was last on). Alt+Enter still switches. If you never changed **Window mode** before, you'll get full screen too; the game's settings (F2) → Display → **Window mode** takes you back to a window.
- The presets in the game's settings (F2) → Display have changed. **Enhanced** is gone, and **Widescreen, light** is now called **Balanced** and sets twice the original resolution. What you set with a preset before stays as it is.
- Long setting names in the settings menu now show in full.

Missions

- Purple Haze
  - Above 30 fps the game stayed on "Loading..." for ever after the truck rammed your van. At 60 fps and up the truck didn't stop behind the van as it does at 30, rolled on and ran over one of the two men just as he appeared behind the black screen, and the game then waited for ever for a line he could no longer say. The mission now plays through at any frame rate.
  - On a widescreen picture the haze only covered the middle and left the sides clear, and it pulled the clock and the weapon icon in from the edge of the screen. It now covers the whole picture.
- Hose the Hoes
  - Above 30 fps the fire truck's water fell far short, so the brothel's fires never went out and the girls' bar ran down. The higher the frame rate, the shorter the water: it reached about 27 m at 30 fps, 14 m at 60 and 7 m at 144. It now reaches as far as on the PS2 at any frame rate, and puts a fire out just as quickly. This goes for the fire truck everywhere, including the Fire Truck side job. Locking the game to 60 fps is no longer needed.
  - The two burning cars parked outside the brothel blew up twice as soon at 144 fps, and their blasts can set rooms you've already put out alight again. They now go off when they do on the PS2.
  - With the mouse, the water drifted off the fire you were aiming at. The hose points where the camera looks, and a moment after you stopped moving the mouse the camera eased back behind the truck and took the water with it. The gamepad's right stick didn't do this. Now the camera stays where you put it while the truck sprays water, and goes back only once the water has stopped (after the delay in the game's settings (F2) → Keyboard: **Car camera return**, 1.5 s by default).
- Snitch Hitch and Kill Phil
  - At 144 fps, "You are getting too far away from the jet" (or "...from the limo") gave you 1.2 seconds to turn back before the mission failed. It's 2.6 seconds again, as on the PS2. A line of dialogue in each mission also came too early.
- Every mission
  - The kind of wait that hung Purple Haze can no longer hang any other mission: if someone dies before their line has loaded, the game carries on without it.

Fixes

- With free aim on and a gun in hand, holding the right mouse button while sprinting made the camera stutter: it kept stopping and starting. It now turns smoothly.
- On foot, turning the camera toward a nearby building with the mouse made it stop dead at the edge of the wall, and it stayed stuck until you turned back. It now keeps turning and moves in closer to Vic, as it always did with a gamepad.
- Helicopters: at high frame rates the rotor blades did their damage far too fast when they touched a wall or anything else. At 144 fps a helicopter was on fire in under a second. The blades now do the same damage per second as on the PS2 at any frame rate.
- Recording with Bandicam, or taking a Steam screenshot, filled the console with hundreds of lines and wrote picture files next to the game; F12 was also a developer key in the game. The game now leaves F12 alone.
- Laptops with two graphics cards: the game could start on the slow integrated one. It now picks the dedicated card. New in the game's settings (F2) → Graphics: **Graphics card** lists the cards your PC has by name, the fastest first, with "(integrated)" after the one built into the processor. The first is the default; the change applies after a restart. This couldn't be tried on a laptop with two cards here, so it's hoped to fix the problem.

Also

- Windows: vcs.exe now has an icon of its own, a daytime version of the launcher's, so you can tell the two apart on the taskbar. Its version and description show in Explorer.
- The launcher keeps this fork's European disc support and its own updates; it is not Apple-signed and there is no Mac release.

What still needs playing through

- Purple Haze, Hose the Hoes, Snitch Hitch and Kill Phil were not played through on the European disc here. Their fixes are the original project's, and every address they use on the European disc was checked against the game's executable; the behaviour itself is hoped to be the same.

Updating

- On Windows, go to Settings → Updates → Update now in the launcher (or download this release and extract it into a new folder), then update the build: it turns your disc into code again and builds the whole game once, so it takes as long as a first build. Your saves aren't touched.
- On Linux, update from Settings in the same way, or download the new AppImage.
- The game itself has changed a lot: if anything looks or sounds wrong, the log next to vcs.exe and the launcher's report are the things to send.
