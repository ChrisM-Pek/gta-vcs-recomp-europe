New: the pause menu with the keyboard and the mouse on the European disc

- The game's pause menu now works as a PC menu on the European disc, as it already did on the American one: Escape opens it, Enter selects (Cross), Escape goes back (Triangle; on the row of tabs it closes the menu). The cursor shows, pointing at an item moves the game's highlight, and a click selects.
- Sliders (Audio > Music / Sound effects volume, Display > Brightness): a click on a bar sets the level, holding the button and moving drags it, the wheel turns it a notch. A click on the label only moves the highlight there.
- Choices (speaker setup, radio station, subtitles, radar, HUD, trails, the Controls page): a click turns to the next one, the wheel turns either way.
- Map: drag it with the left button, the wheel zooms (up: in). Brief and Stats scroll with the wheel. The right button goes back.
- The Save menu a safehouse opens works the same way.
- Before, the European build's menu only took the gamepad (the arrow keys worked as the D-pad, Shift / F as Cross, Escape opened it): the mouse and Enter did nothing inside it. The gamepad is unchanged, and what the menu takes (the mouse buttons, the wheel) doesn't reach the game while it is up.

New: aim and fire from the driver's seat

- With the game's settings (F2) → Keyboard: **Free aim** on, hold the **right mouse button** (or E) while driving a car to aim with the mouse, and use the **left mouse button** to fire. W / A / S / D still drive, and Space is still the handbrake.
- The mouse wheel (the game's settings (F2) → Keyboard: **Mouse wheel**, on by default) cycles through the pistols and SMGs you have ammo for while you drive, whether Free aim is on or off (down: next, up: previous).
- Until now the wheel changed the radio station while you drove. It doesn't any more: **Q** (L1's key) changes the station, as on the PS2.

The frame rate cap

- New in the game's settings (F2) → Display → **Frame rate cap**: **25**. Like the other caps it runs at that rate with **Frame-rate independent** timing, which is the default; with Original (PS2) timing it runs at 30.
- At the 25 cap the frames stay evenly spaced.

Also

- There is no Mac release: the European disc hasn't been tried on a Mac, as before.

Updating: on Windows, open the launcher's Settings → Updates → **Update now**, then **Update the build** under Play: it rebuilds the game (a few minutes), and your disc doesn't need turning into code again. Your saves aren't touched. You can also extract this release into a new folder. On Linux, update from Settings the same way, or download the new AppImage.
